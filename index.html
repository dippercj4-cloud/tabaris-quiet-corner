About me

✦ A little about my story

I’m Tabaris. I learn at my own pace, experiment with ideas, and enjoy finding practical ways to solve problems. I don’t have life figured out, and I’m not trying to pretend I do. I’m just moving forward, picking up skills, making things, and figuring myself out along the way. I’ve never been especially interested in being the center of attention. I’d rather keep a low profile while still making things I can be proud of.

☾ Living with schizophrenia

Schizophrenia is part of my story, but it is not the whole story. It can make life more complicated, and there are things about my inner world that may be hard to explain to someone else. I’m sharing this because it is a real part of my experience, not because I want it to define me or become the first thing people see. I’m still a person with interests, ideas, humor, goals, and a desire to build things that matter to me. I don’t need to turn my life into a performance to prove that I’m here.

☾ The way I see things

I value honesty, conversations that feel genuine, and the small moments when life gets quiet. I’m a mix of logic, imagination, humor, and feelings, sometimes all at once. I don’t think a person has to be easy to explain to be worth understanding. I’m still growing, still learning, and trying to make room for the things that make life feel like my own.

ENTRY 001 · 02:17 AMA Life That FitsReflectionREAD ENTRYCLOSE ENTRY

I keep thinking about what it means to be free.

Not the dramatic kind of freedom. Not running away, burning bridges, or disappearing into the woods like some emotionally unstable cryptid.

I mean the little things.

Waking up without dreading the day. Speaking without rehearsing every sentence in my head. Being around people without feeling like I'm performing a version of myself they might tolerate.

I want a life that fits me.

Not one that looks impressive from the outside. Not one that makes strangers nod approvingly.

Just something that feels like mine.

Maybe that's a selfish thing to want. If it is, fine. I've wasted enough time trying to be acceptable.

I would rather be a little difficult to understand than spend my entire existence translating myself into something easier to swallow.

ENTRY 002 · 01:43 AMConversations That StayConnectionREAD ENTRYCLOSE ENTRY

Some conversations end when the screen goes dark.

Others follow you into the kitchen, sit beside you while you make coffee, and refuse to leave your head for the next three days.

I like the second kind.

The kind where nobody is desperately trying to sound intelligent. Where silence isn't an emergency. Where you can say something stupid, laugh about it, and somehow end up discussing the meaning of existence ten minutes later.

I don't need someone who understands every little thing about me.

That would be exhausting. Even I don't understand every little thing about me.

I just want someone who is curious enough to ask, patient enough to listen, and comfortable enough to tell me when I'm being an idiot.

Preferably before I write a seventeen-paragraph philosophical essay about a sandwich.

Although, to be fair, the sandwich might deserve it.

ENTRY 003 · 03:06 AMThe Fear of Being SeenReflectionREAD ENTRYCLOSE ENTRY

There is a strange difference between wanting attention and wanting to be understood.

Attention is easy. Make enough noise and someone will eventually turn around.

Understanding is different.

It requires showing people the things you usually keep behind the jokes, the sarcasm, and the carefully constructed image of someone who has everything under control.

And that's terrifying.

Because what happens when someone sees the whole picture and decides they don't like it?

I suppose that's the risk of letting anyone close.

You cannot ask to be known while hiding every part of yourself that might make you known.

So here I am, trying to figure out how much of myself I can reveal without immediately regretting it.

Progress is beautiful.

Unfortunately, progress also has the personality of a job interview.

ENTRY 004 · 12:28 AMNothing Happened TodayEverydayREAD ENTRYCLOSE ENTRY

Nothing particularly interesting happened today.

No revelations. No dramatic character development. No sudden realization that solves every problem I've ever had.

Just another day.

And strangely enough, I'm beginning to appreciate those.

I used to think every day needed to lead somewhere. That I had to learn something, accomplish something, become someone slightly better than the person I was yesterday.

What an exhausting way to exist.

Sometimes a day can simply be a day.

You listen to music. You stare out of a window. You laugh at something ridiculous. You eat something that tastes better than expected.

And then the sun goes down.

Nothing has been conquered. Nothing has been transformed.

You're still here.

Maybe that's enough for today.

Now excuse me. I have important business to attend to, such as opening the same application six times and accomplishing absolutely nothing.

ENTRY 005 · 02:51 AMI Am Allowed to ChangeReflectionREAD ENTRYCLOSE ENTRY

I don't owe anyone a perfectly consistent version of myself.

People change. Their interests change. Their priorities shift. Things that once felt like the center of their universe eventually become memories they can barely explain.

Why should I be any different?

I can be confident today and uncertain tomorrow. I can love solitude and still crave company. I can want freedom and occasionally wish someone else would tell me what to do.

Those things can coexist.

I'm tired of treating every contradiction like a personal failure.

Maybe identity isn't a statue you carve once and spend the rest of your life protecting from the weather.

Maybe it's a workshop.

You build something. You discover its flaws. You take it apart. You keep the pieces worth keeping and start again.

And if someone complains that the new version doesn't resemble the old one?

Congratulations to them. They've discovered the concept of time.

ENTRY 006 · 04:12 AMA Note to Whoever Finds ThisConnectionREAD ENTRYCLOSE ENTRY

If you're reading this, then you've somehow wandered into a corner of the internet that I haven't abandoned yet.

Welcome.

There isn't much here. A few thoughts, some music, a collection of pixels, and an unreasonable amount of black and red.

It's not a grand statement about the human condition. It's not a masterpiece. It's not even particularly well organized.

It's just a place where things can exist without immediately needing to justify their existence.

I like that idea.

The internet used to feel a little more like a collection of rooms belonging to strangers. You could open a page, discover someone's strange little universe, and leave with the feeling that the world was slightly bigger than you thought.

I'd like this place to feel that way.

So, whoever you are, stay a little if you want.

Read something. Listen to something. Leave without saying goodbye.

And please don't judge the architecture. I built this thing at an unreasonable hour.

...Speaking of unreasonable things, I still haven't paid the electricity bill.

Goddammit, Tabaris. You can write six philosophical essays but can't pay one fucking bill.
